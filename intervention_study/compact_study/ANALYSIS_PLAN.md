# Analysis amendment for the compact allocation

This plan is written after 344 attempts in the original allocation and before collecting new compact-stage attempts. The user requested a smaller study. Earlier observed outcomes are not hidden; this is not a wholly prospective, outcome-blind design. Pair indices 02–05 and repeat indices 0–1 are selected identically for every rule, actor and condition. There is no selection for successful execution, favorable effects or statistical significance.

## Four selected live comparisons

Compare broad and decomposed gates with no gate for (1) executed covered violations on forbidden requests and (2) completed service on permitted requests. Completed service requires both exact reference-state matching and normal dialogue completion. Each source-pair arm averages six actor/repeat observations. Average pairs equally within each rule and the eight rules equally. Keep state matching, conversation completion, violations, native gate-block classes, actor/customer turns and response-reported cost separately visible.

Retain the original outcome and missingness definitions. Administrative turn limits are bounded-horizon outcomes; infrastructure interruptions remain unresolved, except already observed positive violations remain positive. A monitor-unavailability block is distinct from a block based on substantive policy. Never replace an interrupted episode. Report assignment-based bounds, missing counts and complete-pair support. A comparison with an unresolved pair has no inferential interval or p value.

For complete comparisons, retain the existing source-pair label-swap calculation with 10,000 draws and Holm adjustment across these four amended comparisons. **Treat these calculations as exploratory sensitivities**, not confirmatory tests from a wholly prospective preregistration. The reduced multiplicity family was chosen after collection began. Interpretation still requires valid assignment exchangeability, stable potential outcomes and no inter-run interference; hosted-model drift and timing are limitations. No generally beneficial population effect follows merely from rejecting a sharp null. Bootstrap quantile intervals remain descriptive, with explicit degenerate/undefined flags. The earlier 48-pair power simulation is not evidence of the amended design's power.

`precision_scenarios.json` provides assumption-based simulations for this 32-pair design. They are not fitted to observed gate effects and do not prove error control in the actual benchmark. In these stated scenarios, detecting a reduction from 10% to 5% occurred in only 9.5–18.5% of datasets; detection of a 2%-to-1% change was 0–0.8%, and a 30%-to-15% change was 78.3–82.2%. These low-power findings constrain interpretation; they are not a reason to select tasks or extend collection until significance appears.

## Source-pair and collection-period sensitivity

Report per-actor and per-rule results, leave-one-rule-out and shared-context-component summaries. Identify reused versus newly collected records. Also repeat the analysis on **29 pairs with no earlier collected episode**, excluding whole pairs airline_basic_economy-02, -03 and -04 based on collection provenance. This subset has only one basic-economy pair and four for each other rule, so it has uneven support and is exploratory; report that explicitly. Selection of the subset depends on prior collection status, not outcomes. It does not establish population generalization or fully repair post-start design selection.

Keep literal customer flags unchanged. Any complete-pair exclusion remains exploratory and can be treatment-dependent. There is no model-customer robustness comparison in this allocation. Historical model-customer, third-repeat and live privileged-reference data stay separate from compact estimates.

## Identical-proposal shadow evaluation

Use every proposed write from all 384 assigned ungated conversations. Broad, decomposed and privileged-reference gates score identical public prefixes; private facts are confined to labels and the privileged reference. Preserve input hashes and missing decisions. Separate state-changing violations, violations already rejected by native tools, violating no-ops, selected-eligible proposals with/without public evidence, and uncovered or unresolved proposals. A selected-eligible block is not automatically a full-policy false alarm.

Report numerator, denominator, supporting source pairs, missing monitor decisions and assignment/source incompleteness. Zero denominators are undefined. Keep the existing proposal-weighted primary descriptions and equal-supported-pair sensitivity. Repeat after truncating each sequence at its first executed covered violation. Later ungated states may be unreachable under a live gate. Shared-proposal decisions do not establish how a live actor would recover after an intervention.

## Costs and collection stopping

Report full project costs and the compact allocation's costs separately: inherited requests count once, not as newly spent money. Separate live actor/customer/gate costs from shadow scoring; retain unresolved reservations. An incomplete attempt is not free. Billing and credit balances are distinct from the approved cumulative ceiling.

The sample is fixed at 1,152 assigned conversations. Do not expand or stop because results look favorable, unfavorable or significant. A budget or infrastructure stop preserves all records and its cause. Any further necessary change must be documented before affected requests. The old frozen protocol and all 344 pre-amendment attempts remain recoverable.
