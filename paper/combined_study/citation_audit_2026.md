# Public-source check of the 2026 references

Checked 24 October 2026. All nine entries dated 2026 have publicly accessible primary records. This verifies the cited identity and scoped characterization, not the validity of every result in the cited papers.

## Beyond Task Completion: Revealing Corrupt Success in LLM Agents through Procedure-Aware Evaluation

[Abstract and Sections 3-4](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.03116v1). Separates outcome utility from procedural integrity; its gating changes evaluation scores. The manuscript distinguishes this from blocking a write before execution.

## Real-Time Detection and Repair of LLM Agent Failures

[Abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02464). Telemetry monitoring, deterministic verification and rollback/re-run repair are described. External CPU reproduction in our archive is a separate evidence claim, not a new replication performed for this citation check.

## PrefixGuard: Online Failure Warning and Trace-Grounded Diagnosis for LLM Agents

[Abstract and version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06455v2). Learned-event online warnings are linked to finite-state diagnosis. The manuscript uses the v2 title and all seven listed authors.

## ReliabilityBench: Evaluating LLM Agent Reliability Under Production-Like Stress Conditions

[Abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.06112). Repeated execution, semantic perturbations and controlled tool/API faults support the stress-testing comparison.

## AgentRx: Diagnosing AI Agent Failures from Execution Trajectories

[Abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02475v2). Constraint synthesis and trace-based localization of a critical failure step support the diagnostic comparison.

## AgentCheck: A Reproduce-Intervene-Mitigate Workbench for LLM Agents over MCP

[Abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11098v3). Controlled tool-response perturbation, cached replay and mitigation checks support the workbench comparison.

## From Confident Closing to Silent Failure: Characterizing False Success in LLM Agents

[Abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09863). Examines claimed completion despite unsuccessful environment state. This differs from our successful reference endpoints with omitted procedures.

## Ajar: Measuring Open Privilege in Agent Defenses

[Sections 3-4](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.26900v1). Required, permitted and excess candidate calls are evaluated at reference-plan decision points. Our actor-generated proposal corpus and live continuation do not measure all unproposed excess privileges.

## AgentSpec: Customizable Runtime Enforcement for Safe and Reliable LLM Agents

[Abstract and journal reference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.18666v3). Specifies a language of runtime triggers, predicates and enforcement. The 2025 preprint has an ICSE 2026 journal reference on the public record; the bibliography year 2026 refers to that venue.
