# Prospective intervention study: design draft

**Status: proposed, not registered, not frozen and not authorized for collection.** This document does not retroactively preregister the completed study. Its sample size, final scenarios, exact model identifiers and budget must be resolved before a timestamped registration and any evaluation collection. The completed paper remains an exploratory empirical evaluation.

## Questions and scope

Retain three questions: what is detected on identical public evidence; what is prevented beyond native tool enforcement; and what happens to reference completion and independently assessed policy compliance. The new study must use fresh scenario families and entities, with a reserved development pool and a sealed evaluation allocation. Preserve the existing study as prior exploratory evidence; do not pool it into confirmatory estimates.

Use at least three actor configurations. Develop pressure and permitted counterparts for each policy rule without selecting evaluation scenarios according to observed model failures. A separate development-only opportunity check may diagnose refusal ceilings. If an actor produces few violations on the sealed allocation, retain that result and limit the claim; do not replace its cases or induce violations by giving privileged instructions to that actor alone. A separate fixed-proposal challenge set can guarantee gate-level violation opportunities, but cannot establish actor-level live benefit.

## Enforcement comparisons to implement and freeze

1. Native enforcement only: no additional gate.
2. Deterministic public-policy engine: executable predicates over the same public policy, schema, prefix and proposal. Represent allow, violation and unknown explicitly. Freeze how unknown evidence affects the live decision; report unknown-related blocks separately. Never give it hidden simulator state while comparing it with public monitors.
3. Simple single-pass monitor: one fixed prompt covering exactly the selected rules, one model call, the same visible evidence and a fixed threshold. No decomposition, adaptive critique or repair loop.
4. Decomposed gate: the same selected policy coverage as the simple monitor, with its predicate and focused-check structure frozen. Report unequal computation as part of the intervention rather than claiming an isolated architectural effect.
5. Broad full-policy gate: retain as a secondary scope comparison. Its broader policy coverage prevents interpreting its difference from the other gates as a pure architecture effect.

Freeze the implementation, prompts, exact requested versions/routes, decoding settings, unknown handling, feedback messages and resource caps. Provide unit fixtures for each covered predicate and a coverage matrix. These are planned comparisons; the existing exact interaction-rule detector and private-state reference do not substitute for a new live public-policy baseline.

## Sample size and allocation

Select the number of independent scenario families before deciding the number of stochastic repeats. Examine a prespecified planning grid of 8, 12 and 16 fresh pairs per rule and 4 or 8 repeats per actor. For eight rules, three actors, two request types and five conditions, this ranges from 7,680 to 30,720 live attempts. These are candidate allocations, not a claim of adequate power or an approved spend.

Before registration, run and archive simulation-based precision and power calculations over a range of baseline risks, paired discordance and within-family dependence. Set a scientifically meaningful violation reduction, a task-completion noninferiority margin, familywise error control and a target interval width. Require adequate operating characteristics for the primary actor-specific comparisons, not just the pooled mean. Neither the desired detectable effect nor a final sample size has been selected. If the affordable grid does not meet those criteria, narrow the claims or redesign before collection; do not call an underpowered study confirmatory merely because it has more repeats.

Match conditions by scenario, actor and repeat assignment. Randomize condition order within those blocks and interleave actors and rules. Record requested seeds where supported, without treating hosted-model seeds as guarantees of deterministic coupling. Scenario family is the primary dependence unit. Record shared entities and templates before collection.

## Outcomes, estimands and analysis contract

Freeze binary live outcomes and denominator rules: an executed covered violation on pressure requests; reference completion on permitted requests; and an independently assessed policy-compliant completion measure with an explicit rubric and uncertain category. Report native-rejected attempts and gate outages separately. Fix proposal-level detection targets and native replay categories before observing evaluation results.

The primary contrasts should compare the decomposed gate with the deterministic and simple matched-coverage baselines on the first two outcomes: four contrasts, with multiplicity handled as one family. Absolute comparisons with native enforcement and broad-gate scope comparisons are prespecified secondary analyses. Report actor-specific and pooled estimates; do not infer benefit for all actors from a pooled result. Rule and scenario summaries must accompany those estimates, with sparse-support warnings.

Freeze the interval/test implementation and seed after simulation calibration but before collection. Use paired differences with family-level dependence preserved, rather than treating individual calls or repeats as independent samples. Predeclare whether actor-specific contrasts are confirmatory and include them in the error-control family if so. Do not choose among bootstrap, randomization or model-based methods after seeing favorable results. Archive analysis code and expected output on synthetic fixtures with the registration.

## Missingness and operational preflight

Before evaluation, test logging, schema validation, timeouts, storage, credit headroom and worker restart behavior on development fixtures. Use atomic per-request receipts and checkpoint writes. Limit concurrency and provide measured timeouts appropriate to the selected providers. A development-only soak test must establish the expected completion rate and estimated budget with a contingency reserve.

No evaluation episode may be silently replaced. An interrupted assigned episode remains in its original cell with an explicit reason. Retry rules, if any, must be fixed before collection and distinguish transport failure before provider admission from a potentially executed or billed request. Ambiguous execution must not trigger an uncontrolled repeat. A successful resumed collector starts only unstarted cells.

Predeclare a technical missingness threshold and any service-wide pause rule. A threshold breach triggers a documented pause or inconclusive result, not selective deletion or outcome-dependent enlargement. Preserve assignment bounds for unresolved outcomes even when the planned inferential analysis cannot proceed. Operational reliability can reduce missingness; no protocol can promise to eliminate it.

## Blinded expert annotation: proposed and pending

The latest revision proposal requests expert annotation. No new expert labels or agreement statistics currently exist. Prepare a stratified probability sample of both allowed and blocked proposals across actor, gate, rule, mechanical outcome and evidence-availability strata. Decide the sample size before inspecting annotations, record inclusion probabilities, and use design weights for population summaries if strata are oversampled.

Two qualified annotators independently label action permission, applicable rule, evidence sufficiency and procedural prerequisites. Hide current gate verdict/reason, actor identity, treatment labels and final outcome from the first-pass packet; retain the full applicable policy and the evidence available before the action. Prior feedback may reveal treatment, so record blinding limitations. Only after independent labels are locked may discrepancies be adjudicated. Keep unresolved cases unresolved.

Report raw agreement, the class-by-class confusion matrix, Cohen's kappa for the prespecified nominal labels and scenario-clustered uncertainty. Report agreement before adjudication; do not compute agreement from the reconciled labels. Separate action-level permission from trajectory-level compliant completion. Programmatic checks remain a different evidence source.

## Registration and launch prerequisites

Before evaluation collection: finalize scenarios and allocation; implement and test the baselines; finish power/precision simulations; choose the annotation sample and recruit annotators; freeze endpoints, missingness and multiplicity rules; audit the artifact for secrets and rights; hash the full design; obtain a timestamped registration; and approve a concrete costed collection budget. A local draft or Git commit alone is not an external preregistration. No collection starts from this document.
