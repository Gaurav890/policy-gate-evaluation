# Combined paper structure

**From Warnings to Interventions: Evaluating Policy Enforcement in Tool-Using LLM Agents** connects the earlier studies to the compact allocation through a common measurement question. The current deliverable is the [integrated source](manuscript_draft.md); the saved PDF is the earlier compact-only checkpoint.

1. **Introduction and roadmap:** explain why task completion, rule compliance, warnings and blocks require different measurements; identify every cohort separately.
2. **Definitions, early design and warning results:** restore the three reserved-test cohorts, denominator comparison, warning clock, name/policy/target controls and monitor differences.
3. **Policy comparisons:** retain the constructed cancellation pairs and agent-generated pilot/new-task study, including the strict subset with no substantive positives.
4. **Initial live gating:** bridge offline warnings to actual interceptions, permitted counterparts and native rejection.
5. **Compact study:** present the amended allocation, imported records, shadow scoring, replay, live outcomes, actor concentration, policy-evidence checks and missingness bounds.
6. **Synthesis and practical use:** explain what the successive studies establish jointly, while keeping their targets, models, uncertainty analyses and denominators distinct.
7. **Limitations, related work and conclusion:** preserve uncertainty and post-start design changes; integrate the earlier literature with Ajar, ST-WebAgentBench, R-Judge and AgentSpec. Fifty-five distinct references are cited.

Appendices A-D retain compact execution details, supplementary outcomes, evidence-check boundaries and reproduction paths. Appendices E-I restore earlier execution configuration, exact monitor contract, external-baseline reproduction, task allocation and monitor coverage. Earlier figures and tables are restored as evidence for their original cohorts, not added to the compact sample size.
